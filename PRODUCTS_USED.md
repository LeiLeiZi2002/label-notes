## Unbranded and demonstration material

- `pretest.html` uses a grocery-shopping story with bread, cereal and cracker examples. No brand or real package photograph is specified. The wording is retained from the original pretest; it should not be cited as a complete ingredient list for a named product.
- `assessment.html` scenario 6 is an unbranded claim-reading question.
- `dev-question-types.html` is unlinked and explicitly labeled Demo. Its practice ingredients A and B and fictional label are not real food products and do not contribute to course results.
- The uncured/celery explanation gives a generic labeling example, not a new branded package. The “no artificial preservatives” explanation likewise makes no claim that an existing pictured cracker has that front-label statement.
- Homepage food photography and the course-preview illustrations do not establish any brand’s ingredient list.

## Version and reuse notes for the next question-design round

- The Mariani picture contains potassium sorbate as well as the two sulfiting agents. Current manufacturer text may differ. Use the pictured list, not a blend of different package versions.
- The Mt. Olive picture lists Yellow 5. Do not replace it with a newer turmeric formula while retaining the old picture.
- The reduced-sodium ramen photograph has folded or obscured text at its edges. The text version marks these gaps; the TBHQ wording used by the question is visible. The manufacturer link is supplementary, not a replacement transcription for the old photo.
- Hormel bacon is intentionally used across both chapters. Mariani, apple slices, regular ramen, Cheerios, crackers, pepperoni and mayonnaise are already repeated between learning and chapter checks. This round records that reuse; the next round can select different assessment products.
- Product references can also occur in question wording without an image. Check both `questions` and `blocks` in `assets/questions/*.js` before treating a product as unused.
