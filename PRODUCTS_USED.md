## Unbranded material and version notes

- The nine-stop pretest uses unbranded shopping situations. Its clickable bagel list is labeled “Practice label.”
- Preservatives group 6 uses a fictional package to examine a complete nitrite claim and its exception. It is not paired with a real brand photograph.
- The antioxidant blend in group 1 is a practice label. Production problems are authored scenarios, not factory recipes or claims about a named manufacturer's process.
- The 365 granola ingredient photo is `61hGglybOxL.jpg` from the Whole Foods gallery. Its order matches the displayed transcription. Another photo in that gallery has a different ingredient order and is not used.
- The Kraft mozzarella photograph lists cornstarch, not modified cornstarch. The ranch bottle is 8 fl oz, not 16 fl oz.
- The proposed Oscar Mayer source now shows a different formula with sodium nitrite; it was not used for an uncured/celery claim question.
- Existing teaching photos and classroom practice remain in place. Mt. Olive was removed from the chapter check; its older local photos are retained but unused.
- `dev-question-types.html` is an unlinked demonstration and contributes no course result.
