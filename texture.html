Chapter 03 / Emulsifiers, stabilizers & thickeners

Step 01

Start here

Why homemade vinaigrette separates

Shake oil and vinegar in a jar. It looks mixed at first, then the oil rises again. A creamy dressing stays mixed much longer. Yogurt can collect a watery layer, and ice cream can turn grainy after warming and refreezing. Each is a different texture problem.

Emulsifiers help oil and water stay mixed. Stabilizers and thickeners can give liquids body, hold water in place or keep small pieces from sinking.

Four terms you’ll see in this chapter
Emulsion
Tiny droplets of one liquid spread through another, as in oil and vinegar after shaking.
Emulsifier
An ingredient that helps the droplets stay dispersed.
Thickener
An ingredient that gives a liquid more body.
Stabilizer
An ingredient that helps the texture stay even, for example by slowing settling or ice crystal growth.
Which of these have you noticed in your own kitchen?

Step 02

Ingredients & their uses

Start with ingredients that help mixtures stay together. Then look at what gives foods body, holds water or keeps small pieces from settling.

Part A · Emulsifiers

01Egg yolk

Hellmann’s mayonnaise

Egg yolk keeps fine oil droplets dispersed in the water and vinegar in mayonnaise.

Without it · The mixture separates more readily and can lose its thick, smooth texture.

On the label · Look for Egg yolks. Near the end, calcium disodium EDTA is listed with “used to protect quality.” The two ingredients have different jobs.

Hellmann’s Real Mayonnaise

02Soy lecithin & sunflower lecithin

Hershey’s milk chocolate

Lecithin helps fat coat dry particles, so melted chocolate can flow more easily.

Without it · The chocolate mixture can be thicker and harder to pour into molds.

On the label · Hershey’s lists Lecithin (Soy). Other chocolate labels may list Sunflower lecithin, which names a different source.

Hershey’s Milk Chocolate Bar · Chocolate flow study

03PGPR

Hershey’s milk chocolate

PGPR helps melted chocolate start flowing, which makes it easier to fill a thin mold.

Without it · It can take more force to get the chocolate moving through fine mold details.

On the label · Look for PGPR near Lecithin (Soy). It stands for polyglycerol polyricinoleate.

Hershey’s Milk Chocolate Bar · Chocolate flow study

04Mono- and diglycerides

Jif peanut butter

Mono- and diglycerides help keep peanut oil mixed into the peanut butter.

Without it · In peanut butter made with just peanuts and salt, oil can rise and collect at the top.

On the label · Jif lists Mono And Diglycerides alongside fully hydrogenated vegetable oils. The name describes an ingredient family and does not tell you the trans fat content.

Jif Dark Roast Creamy Peanut Butter · Smucker’s Organic Creamy Peanut Butter

05DATEM & sodium stearoyl lactylate (SSL)

Pepperidge Farm bread

DATEM helps dough hold gas as it rises. Sodium stearoyl lactylate helps keep the crumb soft.

Without it · A loaf may hold less air as it rises or lose softness sooner during storage.

On the label · Look for DATEM, Sodium stearoyl lactylate and Monoglycerides. The same list names calcium propionate and sorbic acid together “to extend freshness.”

Pepperidge Farm Farmhouse Whole Grain White Bread · Ingredient list at Target

06Polysorbate 60 & polysorbate 80

Kraft ranch and Blue Bunny fudge swirl

Polysorbate 60 helps keep oil droplets dispersed in a water-based dressing.

Without it · Oil droplets in the dressing can join up and form a separate layer more readily.

On the label · Kraft ranch lists Polysorbate 60. On the Blue Bunny label, Polysorbate 80 sits within the fudge swirl ingredients.

Kraft Classic Ranch Lite Dressing · Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party

Part B · Stabilizers & thickeners

What does “gum” mean on a food label?

Food gums can come from seeds, seaweed or fermentation. They interact with water, giving foods body or helping the texture stay even.

07Xanthan gum

Kraft ranch dressing

Xanthan gum thickens cold dressing and helps keep chopped herbs and garlic suspended.

Without it · A thinner dressing runs off lettuce faster, and small pieces can sink while the bottle sits.

On the label · Look for Xanthan gum near Garlic. The dressing also lists modified food starch, which adds body.

Kraft Classic Ranch Lite Dressing

08Guar gum, carob bean gum & tara gum

Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party

Seed gums bind water and help limit ice crystal growth in frozen desserts.

Without it · Ice crystals can grow larger as the tub warms and refreezes, giving the dessert a grainy texture.

On the label · Look for Guar gum, Tara gum and Carob bean gum near the end of the list. Carob bean gum also goes by Locust bean gum.

Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party

09Gellan gum

Silk Original Almondmilk

Gellan gum helps keep fine almond solids and added calcium from settling in the carton.

Without it · Fine particles can collect at the bottom, leaving a thinner first pour and a chalkier last one.

On the label · The same list contains ascorbic acid with a freshness note. Gellan gum helps ingredients stay suspended, and ascorbic acid helps protect freshness.

Silk Original Almondmilk

10Carrageenan

Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party

Carrageenan comes from red seaweed and helps stabilize the texture of foods such as ice cream.

Without it · Depending on the recipe, a frozen dessert or dairy drink may lose its even texture more quickly.

On the label · Blue Bunny lists Carrageenan with other gums. Silk describes its almondmilk as free from carrageenan and lists gellan gum in its ingredients.

Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party · Silk Original Almondmilk

11Pectin

Yoplait strawberry yogurt and grape jelly

Pectin forms a soft gel that gives fruit yogurt and jelly body.

Without it · In recipes that use pectin to set, the jelly can run and the yogurt can be thinner.

On the label · Look for Fruit pectin on the Yoplait list. In fruit jellies, it often appears simply as Pectin.

Yoplait Protein Strawberry Yogurt

12Modified food starch / modified corn starch

Yoplait strawberry yogurt and Kraft ranch

Starch adds body and helps hold water. In fruit yogurt, it can work alongside pectin to reduce watery separation.

Without it · Clear liquid can pool on top of the yogurt, and a low-fat dressing can feel thin.

On the label · Yoplait specifies Modified corn starch. Kraft lists Modified food starch without naming a starch source.

Yoplait Protein Strawberry Yogurt · Kraft Classic Ranch Lite Dressing

Step 03

Read a food label

A single ingredient list can include several texture ingredients alongside preservatives. Use this ranch dressing to see where each group appears.

Worked example

Kraft Classic Ranch Lite Dressing

Ingredient-list excerpt

Water, corn syrup, soybean oil, vinegar, egg yolks, salt, modified food starch, sugar … xanthan gum, garlic … polysorbate 60 … potassium sorbate and calcium disodium EDTA (to protect flavor).

Keep the oil mixed

Egg yolks and polysorbate 60 help keep fine oil droplets suspended in the dressing.

Give the dressing body

Modified food starch adds body. Xanthan gum helps keep the chopped garlic and herbs suspended.

Protect freshness

Potassium sorbate slows microbial spoilage. Calcium disodium EDTA binds trace metals that can speed oxidation.

Show ingredient list as text

Water, corn syrup, soybean oil, vinegar, egg yolks, salt, modified food starch, sugar, contains less than 2% of monosodium glutamate, phosphoric acid, xanthan gum, garlic*, artificial color, polysorbate 60, spice, parsley*, onions*, natural flavor (includes milk), lactic acid, potassium sorbate and calcium disodium EDTA (to protect flavor). *Dried.

Step 04

Review & practice

Does floating oil mean the peanut butter is bad?

Oil naturally rises in peanut butter made with just peanuts and salt. Stir it back in. The oil layer on its own does not mean the peanut butter has gone bad.

Smucker’s Organic Creamy Peanut Butter

Why put several gums in one food?

Several gums can work together in small amounts to control texture. Counting the names on a label does not tell you whether the food is safe or healthy.

In yogurt, pectin and starch help hold water. In a frozen dessert, a blend of gums can help keep ice crystals small.

Silk says its almondmilk is free from carrageenan and lists gellan gum, another stabilizer. A claim about one gum leaves room for others in the recipe.

Blue Bunny Peanut Butter Party · Silk Original Almondmilk
